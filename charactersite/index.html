Om-Nom

The Green candy loving Monster.

Challenge for you; eat a piece of candy every time I use the word candy.

A somewhat small introduction to them.

Om-Nom is a character from a small video game company called zeptolab, founded in 2010 by a couple of Russian twins. They developed a video game based on physics puzzles called Cut The Rope. Om-Nom is the main focus of the game in which you have to cut a rope to feed him a candy, hence the name. The physics part comes with the puzzles where you have to cut the rope at specific times and order.

What does Om-Nom do?

He just stands there, patiently, waiting for his candy. He will wait endlessly for that piece of candy. You better not drop it, or hr will be sad, and you don't want to see him sad now do you? Outside of his ingame role, he's a curious wanderer, he is like a pet to his caretaker, which quite frankly, that's what he is. He's always yearning for that sweet taste of the red and gold swirlled candy.

About Om-Nom

He's a candy obsessed green alien who came from a box, his species is not specified outside of being called "Nommies". He is speculated to be a frog, but that has been disproven by zeptolab themselves, simply calling him a cute green monster who loves candy.

Where did Om-Nom come from?

Outside of the box in which he arrived at the doorstep in, there is no mention of his birthplace in any canon media. However, in later seasons of the Om-Nom YouTube web series, he appears to be in a cartoonish enviornment, outside of the real world in season 1. That cartoony world is probably where he comes from. But still it is safer to say he came from a box.
