香港依然堅挺

Hong Kong is standing strong

經濟每天都在成長。

The economy is growing everyday

以下是香港一些主要的產業

here are some of Hong Kong's biggest industries

香港擁有高度發展的自由市場經濟，其特徵包括低稅制、近乎自由港的貿易環境，以及成熟且完善的國際金融市場。香港擁有獨立於中國內地的貨幣－港幣（HKD）。

Hong Kong has a highly developed free-market economy characterized by low taxation, an almost free port trade, and a well established international financial market. They have their own currency separate from Mainland China; the Hong Kong Dollar (HKD).

融資

Financing

香港融資之所以廣受歡迎，是因為它既具備資本自由流動和與國際接軌的規則，又能提供直接、結構化的管道，對接中國內地的市場與資本池。

Hong Kong financing is popular because it combines free capital movement and internationally aligned rules with direct, structured access to mainland China's markets and capital pools.

保險

Insurance

香港的保險業職位相當熱門，這歸因於保費強勁成長、高獲利潛力、為新進人員提供的簽證途徑，以及對數位與專業人才日益增長的需求。

Insurance jobs in Hong Kong are popular due to strong premium growth, high earning potential, visa pathways for newcomers, and expanding demand for digital and specialist talent.

進出口貿易

Import and Export Trade

該產業是當地經濟中規模龐大且根基穩固的支柱——涵蓋超過77,000家機構，吸收了約34萬人就業，並貢獻了近15%的GDP；這一行業的發展，得益於香港作為全球物流、採購及金融樞紐的地位。

The sector is a massive, established pillar of the local economy—employing roughly 340,000 people across over 77,000 establishments and accounting for nearly 15% of GDP—driven by Hong Kong's role as a global logistics, sourcing, and financial hub.
